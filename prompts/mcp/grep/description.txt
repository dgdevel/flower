Search the contents of local files for a regular expression, like
grep -E: every matching line comes back as "path:line number:text",
sorted by path, with a final "[N matches in K of M files]" tally.
Pick the files with the glob argument, which is the list_files
pattern language prefixed with the directory to search —
/dir/*.c (the .c files directly in /dir), /dir/**/*.c (in every
subdirectory too), /dir/file.txt (one file), /dir (every file
under /dir). Binary files and files over 8 MB are skipped,
overlong lines are cut, and the reply stops after 200 matches.
Use it to find where something is defined, used or mentioned
before reading whole files.
